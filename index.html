FAQ

Questions.

How should I allocate my marketing budget across channels? +
The right channel allocation depends on three factors: your industry, your primary goal (awareness vs lead gen vs conversions), and your company stage. A B2B SaaS company focused on lead generation at growth stage should weight heavily toward Paid Search, Content/SEO, and Email — typically 30%, 25%, and 20% respectively. An e-commerce brand focused on conversions should lean toward Paid Social and Paid Search. The Marketing Budget Allocator generates a customized split based on your specific inputs and industry benchmarks.
What percentage of revenue should go to marketing? +
B2B companies typically spend 5–12% of revenue on marketing. B2C and e-commerce companies often spend 10–20%. SaaS companies in growth mode sometimes spend 20–40% of revenue to accelerate customer acquisition. The right number depends on your growth targets, competitive intensity, and unit economics — specifically your Customer Acquisition Cost vs Lifetime Value ratio.
